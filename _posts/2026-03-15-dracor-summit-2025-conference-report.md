A conference report on the **[DraCor Summit 2025](https://summit.dracor.org)** (Berlin, 1–5 September 2025), written by **Laura Untner, Frank Fischer, Henny Sluyter-Gäthje, Julia Jennifer Beine**, and **Peer Trilcke**, has been published on **[H-Soz-Kult](https://www.hsozkult.de/conferencereport/id/fdkn-160945)** (ISSN 2196-5307).

The summit was funded by the Cluster of Excellence Temporal Communities and by DraCorOS. Materials from the summit are archived in the [DraCor community on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/dracor):

- DraCorOS training session "From Dramatic Text to DraCor TEI" (1 September): [slides](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16994544)
- DraCor Corpora Conference (2 September): [all slides in one set](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17251761)
- Second Workshop on Computational Drama Analysis (3 September): [conference reader](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16936633) and [slides reader](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17251555)

Programme and further information: [summit.dracor.org](https://summit.dracor.org)
